Project Name: retail_analytics_v1
Project Description: Retail Store Sales and Customer Lifetime Analytics

Business Requirements:
We operate a retail store with sales data in Snowflake. We need a clean dimensional dbt project.

Sources:
- Database: ECOM_DB
- Schema: RAW
- Tables:
  1. CUSTOMERS (columns: customer_id, first_name, last_name, email, created_at)
  2. ORDERS (columns: order_id, customer_id, order_date, status, total_amount)

Transformations:
1. Staging:
   - stg_customers: Standardize customer names, lowercase email, cast created_at to timestamp.
   - stg_orders: Cast order_id and customer_id to integer, cast total_amount to numeric(10,2).

2. Marts:
   - dim_customers: Customer dimension table containing customer_id, first_name, last_name, email, total_orders, and total_spend.
   - fct_orders: Order fact table containing order_id, customer_id, order_date, status, total_amount, and is_completed flag.

3. Testing:
   - Primary key unique and not_null tests on customer_id and order_id.
